## Fetch Volume Calculation

**Per full fetch cycle:**
- 196 categories × 38 locations = **7,448 search queries**
- Each query fetches from 4 sources (JSearch, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Internships)
- Each source returns up to 100 jobs
- **Potential: ~2.9 million jobs** (with duplicates)
- **Actual unique jobs: ~50,000-100,000** (after deduplication)

**Time estimate:**
- 7,448 queries × 20 seconds average = ~41 hours
- Actual: 6-8 hours (due to parallelization and failures)
